01

NoteIQ

AI-Powered Study System

Students waste hours re-reading passive notes before VTU exams with no way to know what's actually exam-relevant. Built an AI-powered study system that automatically mines PDFs into structured Q&A pairs through RAG pipleline and maps real past-paper questions to answers in a student's own notes — reducing exam prep from hours of re-reading to targeted, active recall. Serves a direct gap for 50,000+ VTU students.

AiGridAlert

Disaster Triage & Dispatch Dashboard

Karnataka's disaster response teams rely on manual triage, slowing critical dispatch decisions during emergencies. Built an AI-powered triage dashboard that classifies incoming field reports across 15 regional disaster categories and recommends the correct rescue unit (SDRF / Fire / Medical) in seconds — replacing a manual, error-prone process with an AI-assisted one that maintains human verification at every step.

03

LexiLearn AI

Smart Vocabulary Quiz Game

Vocabulary acquisition through rote memorisation has a high drop-off rate because learners don't know which words to prioritise. Built a gamified vocabulary system using a spaced repetition algorithm that surfaces difficult words more frequently, automatically compiles a personalised mistake list, and adapts quiz difficulty per user — turning passive word lists into an adaptive learning loop.
