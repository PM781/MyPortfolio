Who I am

More than just code

I'm a CSE student at CMR Institute of Technology, Bengaluru who also studies fine art, competes in international mathematics and linguistics, and builds AI applications because the problems genuinely interest me.

I've taught my class through a pandemic, trained in classical dance for a decade, and somewhere in between, learned Italian and Japanese for no reason other than I wanted to. The rest of it — the art, the math, the six languages — isn't separate from the code. It's all just what happens when curiosity doesn't stay in one place.

I've reached the Finals of the International Youth Math Challenge, qualified in the International Linguistics Challenge, and competed at the national level in the ISTE Ramanujan Mathematical Competition — all while maintaining an 8.4 CGPA with no backlogs.

I also contribute to the CSE department newsletter at CMRIT, and served as class representative for seven consecutive years — including stepping in to teach lessons during COVID-19 when teachers were unavailable.
